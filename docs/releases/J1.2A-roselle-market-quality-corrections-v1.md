# SERAYA — J1.2A Roselle Market-Quality Visual Corrections V1

Status: Implemented directly on `main` after J1.2.

## Intent

Close the first production-facing polish gaps found after J1.2 without reopening the locked guest-invitation architecture or adding new owner data requirements.

## Scope

- strengthen Roselle opening-photo readability across arbitrary owner gallery photography;
- preserve the photo-first story rhythm on the primary mobile guest viewport;
- keep event utility, return, and floating audio controls at a 44 px minimum touch floor;
- continue the editorial mosaic for galleries longer than six images;
- tighten the opening composition on short-height mobile screens;
- align shared and Roselle-specific browser contracts with the locked J1.1/J1.2 opening ritual;
- normalize formatting in the J1.2 files touched by this corrective pass.

## Locked journey preserved

Personal Roselle continues to compose:

1. photo-led opening gate;
2. invitation identity;
3. personal guest greeting;
4. `Buka undangan` action to the couple chapter;
5. couple, story, celebration, gallery, gift, RSVP, Guestbook;
6. emotional farewell and return-to-opening action.

Generic Roselle continues to omit guest-private greeting, RSVP, and Guestbook UI.

## Exclusions

- no migration or Supabase change;
- no new invitation schema field;
- no couple portrait field;
- no standalone wedding-film field;
- no Aruna or Laras visual redesign;
- no WhatsApp automation or delivery behavior change.

## Deployment policy

This corrective pass is delivered as one atomic commit directly to `main` to avoid consuming an additional Vercel branch-preview deployment.
